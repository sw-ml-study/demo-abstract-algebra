Stage 2 lesson 14: subgroups as closed subsets, element order, and cyclic subgroups. In a finite group closure alone suffices, and the lesson both relies on that and records in u:limits that it fails for infinite groups. Element order equals the size of the generated subgroup, asserted as an invariant. Every subgroup found by exhaustion using the n-bit-numeral idiom: Z6 has 4 (sizes 1,2,3,6), S3 has 6 (sizes 1,2,2,2,3,6) -- sizes 4 and 5 never appear, which sets up Lagrange for lesson 15. The visual rings the nine cells where row and column are both members, showing a complete 3x3 Cayley table hiding inside the 6x6 one. New library: u:subset_cells, u:subset_closed, u:is_subgroup, u:power, u:element_order, u:cyclic_subgroup, u:all_subsets, u:all_subgroups, u:subset_size, all mask-based. Gate green: 19 demos, 16 tests.
